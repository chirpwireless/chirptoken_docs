# Claimable Balance

## Chirp Token Locking System Explained

### Why Can’t I Withdraw My Full Balance Immediately?

If you have received rewards in Chirp tokens and want to withdraw them immediately but see a restriction, this is due to the token locking mechanism. This system helps maintain token stability and protects the project’s economy from sudden price fluctuations. It is a common practice in crypto projects to prevent mass sell-offs, which can lead to a sharp drop in token value.

However, this does not mean you are losing your tokens. They gradually become available over time, and you don’t need to take any additional actions.

### How Does It Work?

When you earn rewards in Chirp tokens, they are unlocked gradually:

* 40% are available immediately — you can withdraw them without any restrictions.
* The remaining 60% are partially locked — if you withdraw them immediately, a locking penalty will be applied, meaning you will lose a portion of the tokens.

**However, if you wait, the penalty decreases over time:**

* Immediately after distribution — 60% penalty, 40% available.
* After 1 month — penalty reduced to 40%, 60% available.
* After 2 months — penalty reduced to 26%, 74% available.
* After 3 months (45 epochs) — the penalty for the first epoch is removed, allowing you to withdraw that portion without fees. Further rewards will also gradually unlock following the same logic.

**This means you have two options:**

* Withdraw tokens immediately but lose a portion due to the penalty.
* Wait and gradually receive more tokens without any loss.

If you need funds now, you can withdraw the available 40% or accept the penalty for withdrawing the rest early. However, if you have the patience to wait, you will eventually gain full access to your tokens over time.

### What Are the Benefits?

**Token Stability**

* Prevents sharp price drops and token devaluation.
* Reduces market manipulation and mass sell-offs.
* Maintains a healthy supply and demand balance, benefiting all users.

**Protecting Your Assets**

* Stops large investors from crashing the price with sudden sell-offs.
* Rewards users who stay committed to the project long-term.
* The longer you hold your tokens, the greater your total rewards.

**How Does This Affect Chirp and Kage?**

* If you’re a Chirp user, locking ensures your rewards are protected, supporting long-term token value growth.
* If you’re a Kage player, this mechanism prevents sudden price drops, keeping your rewards valuable.
* Locking encourages long-term participation, strengthening the ecosystem and increasing token value for everyone.

### What Does This Mean for you?

This isn’t a restriction — it’s a way to protect your rewards and increase their value. You still receive your tokens, but full access happens gradually. Each epoch becomes available in sequence: first, rewards from the earliest epochs, then from later ones.

You can choose: withdraw some now with a penalty or wait to unlock your full rewards with no loss.&#x20;
