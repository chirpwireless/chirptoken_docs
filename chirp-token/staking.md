# Staking

## CHIRP Staking: Earn More While You Hold

Staking CHIRP gives you the opportunity to accumulate additional rewards while keeping your tokens. Whether you’re actively involved in mining and looking to put your earnings back to work or simply aiming to generate a steady flow of returns, our Fountain Staking System ensures fair and ongoing reward distribution.

***

### How Staking Works

* You lock your CHIRP tokens for a selected period between 14 and 180 days.
* Rewards are calculated at the moment of staking or claiming and are added to the reward pool.
* Your share of the pool depends on the amount staked and the duration of your stake.
* As more users stake, rewards become more distributed—meaning early participation can be more advantageous.
* The more and the longer you stake CHIRP, the higher your rewards.
* Rewards are continuously distributed and can be claimed at any time without time restrictions.

***

1. Go to Wallet > Staking
2. Click on **Details** of your active staking
3. See total rewards earned so far

**👉 You earn staking rewards consistently since the beginning of staking and can claim them anytime.**

### Withdrawing Staked Tokens Early

1. Go to Wallet > Staking
2. Click on **Details** of your active staking
3. Click **Withdraw Tokens**
4. See penalty details (if withdrawing before the end date)
5. Confirm withdrawal by clicking **Withdraw n CHIRP**

### Why Stake CHIRP?

* **Rewards are continuously distributed and can be claimed at any time without time restrictions.**
* **Flexible lock periods** – Choose any period from 14 to 180 days to fit your needs.
* **Multiple stakes** – Stake different amounts with different durations.
* **Fair participation system** – More stake and longer locks mean a greater share of the pool.
* **Supports CHIRP’s ecosystem** – Strengthens the network and reduces circulating supply.

***
